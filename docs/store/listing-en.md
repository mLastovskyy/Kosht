## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Kosht is a personal finance tracker you will actually keep using. Everything works
offline and stays on your phone: no accounts, no subscriptions, no cloud.

⚡ Log spending in seconds
The calculator opens the moment you add a record: type "12.50+3.90", hit Apply, and
the sum lands in the amount field. Categories sit in a carousel, the date is one tap,
the note is optional.

📸 Receipt scanning, fully offline
Photograph a receipt and the app reads the total, date and store for you. Recognition
runs on the device — photos never leave it.

💳 Accounts: card, cash, whatever you use
One account by default keeps things simple. Add more and Kosht unlocks per-account
balances on Home, an account picker when adding a record or confirming a recurring
payment, and an account filter in Statistics.

📊 Statistics three ways
A category donut, daily bars, a calendar heatmap, and a report for the period you
choose — week, month, quarter or year. It compares with the previous period, shows the
daily average, no-spend days, the top category and plain-language tips.

💱 Live rates and multi-currency
Official National Bank rates refresh automatically. Any foreign-currency amount shows
its BYN equivalent, and every expense freezes its rate at payment time so history never
drifts. Switching the app currency converts every stored amount.

👛 The Wallet
Debts (who owes whom, partial repayments), a savings journal, savings goals with
progress, and recurring payments. Nothing is charged silently: a due payment waits for
your confirmation, and you can correct its amount and rate first.

🏆 Motivation without nagging
A streak of days within your daily budget, custom challenges (spend under X, no-spend,
save X) and 18 awards, from first steps to a 100-day streak. Every award tells you how
to earn it and how far along you are.

🔔 Quiet notifications
An evening reminder (only if nothing was logged), payments awaiting confirmation and a
weekly summary — each with its own switch. No pop-ups over your screen, no forced sound.

🎨 Make it yours
Light and dark themes, Material You dynamic colors, English and Russian, a profile with
a photo or emoji avatar, and toggles that hide anything you do not need.

📖 Built-in guide
A dedicated screen explains every button, plus a full illustrated PDF manual inside the
app.

Privacy: your data never leaves the device. The app reaches the network only for the
National Bank rates and, when you tap it, for an update check.
```
